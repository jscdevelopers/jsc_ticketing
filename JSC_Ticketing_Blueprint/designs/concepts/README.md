# AI-assisted concepts — non-authoritative

The three composite boards in this folder are creative visual explorations, **not** screenshots of JSC operations. They can include synthetic names, made-up statistics and even foreign place names from image generation. **Do not treat any of those as JSC organizational or geographic data, and do not copy them into the production application.**

Use `../vector/04_dashboard_screen.svg`, `../vector/05_ticket_screen.svg`, and `../vector/06_workflow_wizard_screen.svg` as the precise, editable interface references; they intentionally use neutral example station names and JSC-specific access principles.
